# Cover prompts — LAST TRAIN, FIRST SNOW

Three prompts: cover plate, clean plate, loop. Title text is added afterward as a layer
and never generated.

## 1. Cover plate

> A painterly 2D illustration with visible brush texture and a muted print-like grain, in
> the PLATFORM FOUR style. Same recurring character: a calm man in his early 30s, short
> dark brown slightly overgrown hair, unhurried and faintly tired expression, wearing a
> weathered olive canvas jacket over oat-colored knitwear, with a green enamel thermos in
> one hand and a canvas rucksack on one shoulder, positioned on the left third of the
> frame, standing near the platform edge watching the line disappear into falling snow.
>
> Background: a low concrete regional platform with a chipped painted edge, a single
> cast-iron lamp standard throwing warm light down through falling snow, a plain wooden
> name-board with no readable text, a corrugated shelter roof carrying a thin first layer
> of snow, ballast and rails dusted white, bare hedgerow and open fields beyond, dark
> blue-grey dusk sky with the last cold light low on the horizon.
>
> Color palette: moss green, slate blue, oat cream, rust orange and snow white — no neon,
> no saturated primaries, no teal-and-orange grading.
>
> Composition: character on the left third, generous clean negative space across the right
> 42% of the frame for title text. Background fills at least half the image.
>
> No generated lettering, no readable signage, no logo, no watermark. No extra foreground
> characters. No grand terminus station, no steam locomotive, no brass-and-sepia period
> styling, no holiday decoration. Low directional winter dusk light, soft shadows, cold
> air with warm lamp glow. 16:9 thumbnail composition.

## 2. Clean plate (no character, for the loop video)

> 16:9 clean textless painterly 2D illustration background plate for a seamless loop
> video: a low concrete regional railway platform at winter dusk, chipped painted platform
> edge, a single cast-iron lamp standard throwing warm light through falling snow, a plain
> wooden name-board with no readable text, corrugated shelter roof under a thin first
> snow, ballast and rails dusted white, bare hedgerow and open fields beyond, dark
> blue-grey sky with low cold light. Moss green, slate blue, oat cream, rust orange and
> snow white. No title, captions, logo, readable signage, characters, or watermark.

## 3. Loop

> Create a 12-second seamless loop. Locked eye-level camera, no cuts, no zoom, no parallax
> jump. Animate only slow falling snow, a barely perceptible flicker in the lamp, and one
> faint drift of snow along the platform edge. Keep the platform, name-board, shelter,
> rails, hedgerow, sky, and light direction completely stable. First and last frames must
> match. No text, logo, fake readable signage, sudden weather change, passing train, or
> dramatic action.

## 4. Title layer (added afterward)

```
LAST TRAIN, FIRST SNOW
WINTER FOLK · PLAYLIST
```

- Set in a real typeface as a separate layer over the reserved right 42%
- No episode number on the thumbnail
- Changing the episode title later requires no regeneration of the art

## Season checklist

- [x] **Foliage** — bare hedgerow, no leaves
- [x] **Light** — low, directional, cold; soft shadows rather than hard summer ones
- [x] **Clothing weight** — canvas jacket over knitwear
- [x] **Seasonal props** — thermos reads as warmth; first snow, not deep drifts
- [x] **Track subjects** — every title in the episode is winter or neutral; nothing green,
      nothing about warm rain

## Grounding checklist

- [x] Specific nouns: chipped platform edge, cast-iron lamp standard, wooden name-board,
      corrugated shelter roof, ballast
- [x] Landmark excluded: no grand terminus, branch line only
- [x] Background carries more than half the frame
- [x] Character on the series' fixed side (left)
