# Character bible — PLATFORM FOUR

Copy the **Prompt block** verbatim into every cover prompt. Do not paraphrase it.

## Identity

| Field | Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Age band | Man in his early 30s | Not "young man" — the band is the point |
| Hair | Short dark brown hair, slightly overgrown, no styling | Reads as a shape, not a hairstyle |
| Expression | Calm, unhurried, faintly tired in a contented way | Never smiling at the viewer |
| **Fixed prop** | A green enamel thermos, held or clipped to the bag | The silhouette element that survives at thumbnail size |
| Supporting props | Canvas rucksack, paperback book, folded paper timetable | Occasional, not every episode |
| Clothing impression | Weathered olive canvas jacket over oat-colored knitwear | Color family, not a specific garment |
| Frame position | **Left** third of the frame, every episode | Title space is therefore always on the right |

> The thermos does the work. At thumbnail scale the face is roughly 40 pixels tall and
> unrecognizable; the green cylinder against olive canvas is what makes an episode
> identifiable in a subscription feed.

## Prompt block

```
Same recurring character: a calm man in his early 30s, short dark brown slightly
overgrown hair, unhurried and faintly tired expression, wearing a weathered olive canvas
jacket over oat-colored knitwear, with a green enamel thermos in one hand and a canvas
rucksack on one shoulder, positioned on the left third of the frame.
```

```
Color palette: moss green, slate blue, oat cream, rust orange and snow white — no neon,

```

## Style

```
A painterly 2D illustration with visible brush texture and a muted print-like grain, in
the PLATFORM FOUR style.
```

## Visual rules

Always true:

- The character is on the left third; the right 42% stays clean for the title
- Background fills at least half the frame — the place is the subject, the person is the guide
- Practical railway infrastructure: concrete platform edges, cast-iron lamp standards,
  wooden name-boards, corrugated shelter roofs, ballast, rails
- Light is low and directional, matching the episode's hour

Never appears:

- No generated lettering, no readable signage, no logo, no watermark
- No extra foreground characters
- No famous or grand terminus stations — this series is branch lines and request stops
- No steam-locomotive nostalgia, no brass-and-sepia period styling
- No holiday decoration, even in winter episodes
