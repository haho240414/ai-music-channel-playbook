## Listener experience

Music for a slow journey home in cold weather. The listener should feel an unheated
waiting room, a window fogging over, fields going white outside, and the unhurried
patience of a train that stops everywhere. Warm rather than bleak — the cold is outside,
the music is the inside of the coat.

Someone who has never heard of this channel should be able to put it on to work or read
to and never notice there is a series around it.

## Sound ratio

**Shared identity (75%)** — fingerpicked steel-string acoustic guitar, felt piano,
brushed drums played quietly, upright bass, one warm analog pad underneath, subtle tape
texture. Unhurried phrasing with real space between phrases.

**Episode color (25%)** — winter air: slightly dampened high end as though heard through
a coat, longer reverb tails on the piano suggesting an empty carriage, occasional low
rumble under the groove for track movement, brighter guitar harmonics for snow light.

## Global prompt

Appended to every track prompt in this episode.

> Instrumental slow-travel folk, no vocals, no spoken word, no choir, no whistling, no
> bluegrass banjo breakdown, no celtic reel, no country twang, no cinematic trailer build,
> no EDM drop. Fingerpicked steel-string acoustic guitar, felt piano, quietly brushed
> drums, upright bass, one warm analog pad, subtle tape texture. Make the first 5–10
> seconds musically attractive and immediately playable. Keep the track complete,
> unhurried, warm, and quietly hopeful.
