description: Write cover plate, clean plate, and loop prompts for an episode
---

Read `docs/02-thumbnail-prompting.md`, `templates/character-bible.md`, and
`examples/03-character-bible.md` plus `examples/04-thumbnail-prompts.md`.

If the channel has no character bible yet, write one first — it gets pasted verbatim into
every cover prompt from now on, so it must be worth reusing.

Produce three prompts:

1. **Cover plate** — style, character bible verbatim, grounded scene, season and light,
   composition with the title-safe zone, palette, negative list, 16:9
2. **Clean plate** — no character, no text, for loop backgrounds
3. **Loop** — name exactly what may move; declare everything else stable

Then give the title layer text separately.

Non-negotiable:
- Always include `no generated lettering, no readable signage, no logo, no watermark`
- Never put the episode number on the thumbnail
- Ground the scene in two or three specific architectural or environmental nouns, and
  exclude the famous landmark
- Match the season to the music, item by item
